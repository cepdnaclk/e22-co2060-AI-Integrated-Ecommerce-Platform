# I-Computers Platform Guide

## Products and Variants
Our platform organizes items into Products and Variants. A Product is a general item (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro). A Variant is a specific configuration (e.g., Black 256GB).
Multiple sellers can offer the same variant at different prices.

## Seller Offers
Sellers list their stock through "Seller Offers". Each offer specifies the price, stock quantity, warranty, and delivery options for a particular product/variant.

## Order Lifecycle
Orders progress through the following statuses:
1. `pending`: Checkout completed, payment pending or being verified.
2. `confirmed`: Payment successful, seller needs to fulfill.
3. `shipped`: Seller has handed over the item to the courier.
4. `delivered`: Customer has received the item.
5. `cancelled`: Order cancelled.

## Payments
We use PayHere for processing payments. Prices are listed in LKR.

## Inventory
When an order is confirmed, stock is deducted from the seller's offer. Inventory logs track every addition or deduction of stock.
