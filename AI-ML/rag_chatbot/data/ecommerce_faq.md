# I-Computers FAQ

## How to check product availability?
You can search for a product on the platform. The stock level is updated in real-time. If it's out of stock, it will be marked as "Out of Stock".

## How to track an order?
You can go to your account and click on "My Orders". The status will be shown as pending, confirmed, shipped, or delivered.

## Can I compare prices?
Yes! Since multiple sellers can list the same product, you will see all available seller offers on the product details page, allowing you to choose the best price and warranty.

## How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery options may be available from specific sellers and usually take 1-2 business days.

## What is the return policy?
Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Please check the seller's specific warranty details on the product page.
